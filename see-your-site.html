Questions

Things people ask

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We build it from what's public about your business and send you the link. If you don't want it, ignore the email and it comes down after a few weeks. No card, no account.

Why is it so cheap?

We built the tools that do most of the work, and we'd rather have every business in town than a few big contracts. The $99 a year covers the domain, hosting and small fixes. There's no monthly fee and nothing you have to upgrade to.

I already have a domain or a Facebook page.

We'll use your domain, and we link your Facebook page from the site. A Facebook page is good; a real site with your hours, address and phone is what Google shows first.

Can I change things myself?

Yes. Your login lets you change words, photos, hours, menu items and prices, add products and post photos, from a phone or a computer. Or send us a note and we'll do it; small fixes are included.

Do I own it?

The domain is yours. The words and photos are yours. If you ever leave, we hand over the domain and a copy of the site.
