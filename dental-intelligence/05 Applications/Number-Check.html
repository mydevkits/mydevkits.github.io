Why can't this just tell me yes or no?

Because the answer does not live in the number. It lives with our phone provider.

Every phone number belongs to a small local calling zone, usually a town or part of a city. Our provider can accept numbers out of some zones and not others, depending on which local phone companies they have agreements with. That is why a number can be movable in one town and impossible in the town right next to it.

Nothing about the number itself tells you which zone it is in or whether we cover it. Two numbers with the same area code can have completely different answers. The only way to know is to run the number against our provider's system.
