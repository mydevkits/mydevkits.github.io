The story

Remember MySpace?

Nobody "knew how to code." And then, one profile at a time, everyone kind of did. People copied little scripts onto their pages: music players, glitter text, custom backgrounds. Without ever taking a class, millions of people learned the fundamentals of building by building on what already existed.

That moment is happening again, and it's a hundred times bigger. AI can now write, run, test, and fix real programs (trading bots, dashboards, games, automations) while you steer. The only thing missing is the instruction manual.

That's what a MyDevKit is: the instruction manual. Every kit walks you from an empty folder to a finished, running program in numbered steps, with copy-paste prompts, achievements along the way, and nothing vital skipped. You don't need to know how to code. You need to know what you want.
