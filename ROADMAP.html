The strategic picture

Read this first

You are one person running two companies at once. That's the core tension — and the plan.

Track one is income now: land a remote support / success role. Your job-hunt machine is already live and, unusually, is itself the work sample — a recruiter talking to your agent is watching you demonstrate the exact skills the job needs. That's a real edge, and it's 90% built. The missing 10% is what's blocking it.

Track two is MyDevKits, the company: a genuine product line (a flagship voice-AI engine plus three kits) with a live storefront collecting waitlist emails. The danger here is spreading yourself across four half-finished products instead of taking one all the way to a first paying customer.

The recommendation: Finish the job-hunt track this week — it's nearly done and it's your income floor. Then pick one MyDevKits product to carry to a first sale (the flagship engine or the social kit), and let the others hold steady. Depth over breadth is how a solo founder wins.
