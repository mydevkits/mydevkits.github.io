Privacy policy

Last updated 17 August 2026

This describes exactly what MyDevKits, LLC collects, why, and what we never touch. It is short because we collect very little, and specific because a vague privacy policy is worth nothing to you.

The short version. We hold your email address, a record of what you downloaded, and an anonymous number that tells us your copy is running. Our software never sends us your API keys, your posts, your customers, your messages or your files. Those stay on your machine.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhenWhy
Email addressYou enter it to claim or buyTo send you the download, your claim code, and support replies
Which channels you confirmedWhen you claim the free kit To know the free copy was claimed properly
Download recordsEach time you downloadTo know how many copies are out there
An install ID and version numberWhen the software runs To know how many copies are actually in use, and which version, so we know what to support

The install ID is a random number your own computer generates. It is not derived from anything about you or your machine, and it means nothing to anyone else.

What our software never sends us

  • Your API keys. They are stored in a vault folder on your own computer, outside the program folder, and are never transmitted anywhere except to the platform they belong to
  • Your posts, drafts, images or videos
  • Your messages, comments or contacts
  • Your customers' data of any kind
  • Anything about your Page, channel or account beyond what you see on your own screen

This is not a promise about our intentions, it is how the software is built. The kits talk directly from your computer to Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube and your AI providers. We are not in the middle of that.

If the software cannot reach us

It carries on working. The check in is one small message that fails quietly. Nothing in the product depends on our servers being up, and we designed it that way deliberately, because your business should not stop when ours has a bad day.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Paddle, who act as the merchant of record. They take your payment details and we never see them. We receive your email address and the fact that you bought something. Paddle's own privacy policy covers what they hold.

Our website

mydevkits.com is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare, both of which keep ordinary server logs including IP addresses, for security and abuse prevention. We do not run advertising trackers.

If you talk to the assistant on our site, the conversation is processed by our AI providers to produce a reply, and we keep it so we can improve the answers. Do not type anything into it you would not want kept.

Who else sees your data

Nobody, except the services we need to run the business: Paddle for payment, our email provider to send you the link, and our hosting provider. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for advertising, and there is no scenario in which we would.

How long we keep it

Your account record for as long as you have one. Install check ins for two years. Ask us to delete any of it and we will, and tell you when it is done.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you, and we will tell you
  • Ask us to delete it, and we will
  • Ask us to correct it, and we will
