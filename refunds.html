Refund policy

Effective 18 August 2026 · Last updated 18 August 2026

Thirty days, no questions asked. Email us within thirty days of buying and we refund you in full, even if you installed it, used it, and posted with it. You do not have to justify yourself.

How to ask

Email mydevkitshq@gmail.com from the address you bought with, and say you want a refund. That is the whole process. You will get a reply from a person, usually the same day.

Refunds are processed by Paddle, who handled your payment. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with, and usually appears within five to ten business days depending on your bank.

What a refund does

It ends the licence. Your money goes back, and in return your right to use the software stops, the download in your account closes, and updates and support stop with it. Carrying on using it after a refund is use without a licence, and it is the one thing we would take seriously.

We are not going to come to your house and check. We are telling you plainly what the deal is, because a refund policy that pretends the software vanishes is not honest, and neither is one that stays silent about it.

Why we offer this on software you can keep

Because the alternative is worse. Downloadable software cannot really be returned, so the only honest choices are to refuse refunds or to trust people. We would rather trust people and lose the occasional sale than argue with somebody about whether they deleted a folder.

If the kit is not right for you, we would rather you had your money back and thought well of us.

After thirty days

Ask anyway. If something is genuinely broken, or the software cannot do what we said it does, we will sort it out regardless of the date. The thirty days is a promise, not a wall.

Things worth knowing before you buy

None of these are refund conditions. They are the things people are annoyed about later, so we would rather say them now.

  • The Social Presence Kit is Windows software. It does not run on Mac or Linux
  • The Sales and Support Engine needs a server you rent, around $20 a month from any provider, and that is not included
  • Both run on your own API keys from Facebook, Google and others. Setting those up takes an hour or two the first time, and we walk you through every step
  • X charges per post as of February 2026. That is X's pricing, not ours, and it applies whatever software you use
  • These are tools, not a service. You run them. If you wanted somebody else to run it for you, that is a different product and we will say so honestly rather than sell you this one

Chargebacks

Please email us first. A chargeback costs us a fee on top of the refund and takes months, and we would have just refunded you. We have never refused anyone.
