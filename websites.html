Questions

Things people ask

Is it really $200? What's the catch?

It's $200 because we've built the tools that do most of the work, and because we'd rather have every business in town than a few big contracts. The yearly $99 covers real costs (your domain and hosting) plus small changes. There's no monthly fee and no upsell you need to say yes to.

What if I already have a domain?

We'll use it. Either move it over to us so renewal is covered, or point it at the new site. Either way takes a few days and we walk you through it.

Can I change things myself?

Yes. Your login lets you change words, photos, hours, menu items and prices, and add products, from a phone or a computer. Changes show on a preview first; you push them live yourself or send them to us to check.

Is it more than one page?

Most local business sites work best as one well-organized page: people see your hours, menu and number without hunting. If you need more pages (a big menu, a page per service), ask; it's coming as an option.

What about my Google map listing?

That's the free Google Business Profile, and it matters more than anything for "near me" searches. We include a checklist and walk you through claiming it so it matches your site.

Do I own it?

The domain is yours. The words and photos are yours. If you ever leave, we hand over the domain and a copy of the site.
